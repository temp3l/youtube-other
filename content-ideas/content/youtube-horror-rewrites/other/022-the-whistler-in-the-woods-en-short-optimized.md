# Episode 022 — The Whistler in the Woods Was Getting Closer

## YouTube Short Narration

The ranger gave Laura one warning before she entered the forest.

“If you hear a whistle nearby, it is far away. If it sounds distant, it is already close.”

Laura laughed.

Then she heard three faint notes between the trees and whistled them back.

The answer came from directly behind her.

That night, the same tune played through an unused radio frequency. Trail markers had turned toward a restricted valley.

Laura stopped trusting her ears.

She pressed one hand against the ground and felt heavy footsteps circling her camp, even though the whistle sounded kilometres away.

Then her supervisor’s voice came through the radio.

“Laura, rescue is waiting downhill.”

He used her private nickname.

But her supervisor was not working that week.

A faceless shape stepped between the trees. A narrow opening in its chest whistled the same three notes.

Laura escaped by running toward the loudest sound.

Days later, the tune came through an office ventilation grille.

It was almost silent.

Which meant it was standing very close.
