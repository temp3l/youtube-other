# Episode 021 — Something Was Watching From the Bedroom Window

## YouTube Short Narration

Paul’s bedroom was on the second floor.

There was no balcony, tree or ladder.

Yet every night at 2:11, something tapped on the window.

Paul installed a camera.

The recording showed a pale, hairless figure crouched outside, copying his breathing exactly.

When Paul looked directly at it, the creature disappeared.

But in the mirror, it was already inside.

He covered every reflective surface and placed a mannequin beneath the blanket. Then he hid downstairs and watched the camera.

At 2:11, the wardrobe opened.

The creature crawled out, crossed the ceiling and passed through the closed window.

That was when Paul understood.

The tapping was never something trying to enter.

It was the creature leaving his room before he woke.

Months later, a camera beneath Paul’s bed recorded him sleeping.

The camera slowly slid toward his face.

Paul opened his eyes and stared into the lens.

Behind him, something pale was wearing his expression.
