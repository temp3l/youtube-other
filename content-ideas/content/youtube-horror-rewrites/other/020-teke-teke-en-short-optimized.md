# Episode 020 — She Heard Teke Teke Behind Her

## YouTube Short Narration

After the last train passed, Nina heard something scraping behind her.

*Teke... teke... teke.*

She turned and saw a woman pulling herself across the platform with both arms.

There was nothing below her waist.

Nina ran.

The scraping matched every step.

A station worker had warned her about the woman killed on those tracks. If the spirit asked where her legs were, Nina must never hesitate.

The voice came from directly behind her.

“Where are my legs?”

Nina fired her camera flash and sprinted toward a missing memorial plaque she had found beside the tracks. She forced it back into place.

The scraping stopped.

Police found Nina unconscious the next morning. Every photograph showed her running alone.

Except the final image.

It had been taken from ground level behind her.

Two pale hands were visible at the bottom of the frame, dragging the camera forward.

Nina still hears the scraping near railway tracks after midnight.

But now it starts ahead of her.
