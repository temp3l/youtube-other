# Episodes 020–023 — English and German YouTube Shorts

This package contains eight production-ready narration scripts:

- Episode 020 — Teke Teke (English and German)
- Episode 021 — The Rake at the Bedroom Window (English and German)
- Episode 022 — The Whistler in the Woods (English and German)
- Episode 023 — The Vanishing Hitchhiker (English and German)

## Editorial approach

Each short was rewritten from the supplied full story rather than mechanically shortened. The scripts use:

1. An impossible event within the opening seconds
2. One memorable supernatural rule or contradiction
3. Escalation in nearly every narration beat
4. Minimal exposition
5. A final reveal or reversal
6. Approximately 48–60 seconds of narration at a controlled horror pace
