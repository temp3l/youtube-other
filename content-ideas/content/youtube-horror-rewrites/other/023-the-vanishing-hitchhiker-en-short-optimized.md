# Episode 023 — The Hitchhiker Vanished From the Back Seat

## YouTube Short Narration

Felix picked up a young woman walking alone in heavy rain.

Her coat was completely dry.

But water began dripping onto his locked back seat.

She asked him to drive toward an isolated farmhouse. As they approached a broken white cross, she suddenly whispered:

“Do not stop.”

Another version of the same woman stood in the road, soaked and staring into the headlights.

Felix swerved.

When he looked back, his passenger was gone.

The doors were still locked.

Only an old photograph remained in a pool of water.

At the farmhouse, an elderly woman recognised the picture.

It showed her daughter, who had died at that bend decades earlier.

The photograph had been buried with her.

The ghost had not wanted a ride home.

She had brought Felix back to prevent another crash.

He stopped a family moments before a lorry crossed into their lane.

Later, their child told police:

“The wet woman sat beside him the whole time.”

Felix still drives that road.

Whenever his back seat becomes wet, he knows someone is about to die.
