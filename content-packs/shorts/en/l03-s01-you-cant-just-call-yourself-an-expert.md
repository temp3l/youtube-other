Changing your bio to “expert” does not make the market see you as one. Recognition comes from evidence. Does your offer match the expertise you claim? Does your content show that you understand the problem? Does your website make your role clear? Are you present in conversations where that expertise matters? Positioning is the accumulation of these signals. You do not need to look huge or famous. You need enough coherent evidence for people to reach the conclusion themselves. The strongest form of expert positioning is when other people start using the label before you do.
