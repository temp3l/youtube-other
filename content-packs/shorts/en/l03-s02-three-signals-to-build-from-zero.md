Starting from zero? Build these three signals before you chase followers. First: a clear offer. People should understand the problem you solve. Second: useful content. Let them see how you think before you ask them to buy. Third: relevant context. Show up where the people and conversations in your field already exist. You do not need a huge audience or a complex website. At the beginning, alignment beats volume. Ten coherent signals around one expertise can build more recognition than fifty unrelated posts.
