You can be better than your competitors and still lose the client. Why? Because customers cannot see expertise directly. They see your offer, your content, your website, what other people say about you, and what they remember you for. Being an expert and being perceived as an expert are two different problems. Your work builds the expertise. Your positioning makes that expertise visible. If nobody understands what you are especially good at, they have no reason to choose you for it. The goal is not to look impressive. The goal is to make real expertise easy to recognize.
