Want people to know you for something new? Do not start by posting more. Start by defining the old association. What do people think of when they hear your name today? Then define the new expertise, explain the bridge from old to new, and align your offer, site, content, and collaborations around it. Finally, listen to the language people use. When inquiries and introductions begin using the new category, repositioning is working. The goal is not more content. It is a different association.
