If you constantly ask, “What should I post today?”, you probably have a content problem caused by a positioning problem. Start somewhere else: what do you want people to remember you for? Choose one core expertise. List the recurring problems inside it. Then turn each problem into explanations, mistakes, examples, questions, stories, and conversations. Now your content can vary without your professional identity changing every day. Good positioning gives content boundaries. Those boundaries are useful because every new post can strengthen an association that already exists instead of starting from zero.
