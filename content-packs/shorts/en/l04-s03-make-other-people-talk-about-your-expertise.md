One of the strongest positioning signals happens when you are not in the room. Someone asks, “Do you know anybody who can help with this?” and another person says your name. Content can help create that moment. Explain your field clearly, interview people from adjacent areas, participate in relevant conversations, and make your knowledge easy to share. The goal is not only to tell people what you do. It is to build such a clear association between you and a problem that other people can repeat the association for you. That is when positioning starts becoming reputation.
