Open your website and pretend you have never seen your business before. Can you understand, within five seconds, who it is for and what problem it solves? If not, adding more sections may not help. Positioning needs a clear first impression. Your website does not have to explain your entire career on the first screen. It needs to give the visitor the right category. Who do you help? With what? Why should they keep reading? A simple one-page site with a coherent message can position you better than a beautiful site that leaves people guessing.
