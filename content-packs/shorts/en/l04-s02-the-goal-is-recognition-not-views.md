A viral post can give you attention without giving you positioning. The better question is not only, “How many people saw this?” Ask, “What did they learn to associate with my name?” Positioning content repeatedly connects three things: a topic, your name, and useful knowledge. When that happens often enough, people can retrieve you from memory when the relevant problem appears. Views measure exposure. Recognition measures whether that exposure built something useful. You do not need every post to go viral. You need enough people to remember the right thing about you.
