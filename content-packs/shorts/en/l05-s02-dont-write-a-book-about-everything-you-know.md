The fastest way to weaken a positioning book is to put everything you know into it. Marketing, productivity, motivation, branding, mindset, sales, communication — suddenly the reader has no idea what expertise the book is supposed to represent. A stronger book usually does the opposite. Choose one problem, one reader, and one professional territory you want to become associated with. You can explore that subject deeply through stories, examples, mistakes, and methods without losing the position. A book becomes useful for positioning when somebody can easily explain why they should recommend it — and why your name belongs with that topic.
