Try this positioning test. Ask yourself what you want to be known for. Then ask a client or colleague to describe what they think you are known for, without showing them your answer. If the two answers match, your positioning is communicating clearly. If they do not, that gap is valuable information. It tells you what the market currently sees. Positioning is not what you intended to communicate. It is what people actually remember. And when the right problem appears, memory matters because the person they remember is often the person they contact.
