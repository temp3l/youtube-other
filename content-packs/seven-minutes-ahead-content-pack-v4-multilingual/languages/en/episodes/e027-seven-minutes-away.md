Ethan’s missing sister appears on the other side of a seven-minute gap—and she can see him.

The annex room flickers. For twelve seconds, Clara is standing where an empty chair was a moment earlier.

Maya checks the screen again. Nobody can tell whether they are preventing the future or completing it.

She looks older. Exhausted. Completely real.

Ethan reaches out. His hand passes through hers like cold static.

“Clara?”

She starts crying. “You found me.”

Maya checks the countdown. Ten seconds.

“Where are you?” she asks.

Clara looks at Maya as if recognizing someone she has feared for years.

“Not where. When.”

The room begins separating again. Ethan begs her to stay.

Clara grips the invisible boundary between them.

“Ethan, listen to me. After tonight, you’re going to remember something you haven’t done yet.”

Then she looks straight at Maya.

“Do not trust him after tonight.”

The clock hits zero.

Clara disappears.
