Maya watches her boyfriend die on her phone—seven minutes before it happens.

At 8:00 PM, an app she never installed opens by itself. The video shows Ethan stepping off the curb at 8:07. A van hits him.

Maya can feel the room tightening around that fact.

“Very funny,” Maya says—until she notices Ethan is wearing the same jacket. Outside the window, the same bus passes the same torn poster from the clip.

Ethan grabs his keys. “I need air.”

Maya checks the countdown: 01:12. She races after him, tackles him away from the curb, and the van explodes through the exact spot from the video.

Ethan stares at her. “How did you know?”

Maya’s phone vibrates. Another future video begins. This time Ethan is kneeling in a parking garage, his hands covered in blood.

Beside him lies a woman Maya has never seen.

And the timestamp says 8:14.
