Someone stood beside Maya’s bed last night—and her locks were never opened.

Daniel checks the apartment door, windows, hallway camera, and smart-lock logs. Nothing. No entry. No tampering.

Maya cannot tell whether the warning is protection or bait.

“The photo is real,” Ethan says. “So how did they get inside?”

Maya studies it. Beneath the bed, a tiny green reflection catches the flash.

They flip the mattress and find a device no bigger than a coin fixed to the frame. No battery compartment. No wireless identifier.

Daniel bags it. The moment Maya’s phone receives a new Signal, the device pulses.

Once.

Twice.

Perfectly synchronized.

Nora backs away. “I’ve seen one before.”

Maya turns. “Where?”

Nora doesn’t answer. Daniel checks the device clock: seven minutes ahead of every other clock in the room.

“That isn’t a camera,” she finally says. “It’s a relay.”

The device pulses again.

This time Maya’s phone is still dark.
