# Series Bible — 7 MINUTES AHEAD

## Premise
Every evening Maya Vale receives a short video showing events seven minutes in the future. The images are real, but their framing is often manipulative. What begins as a survival mystery becomes a conflict over prediction, free choice, memory, and a system called AHEAD.

## Core cast
Maya Vale — protagonist and Version 17. Ethan Cole — Maya's partner, connected to Clara. Nora Reyes — Maya's closest friend and former AHEAD field observer. Daniel Cross — detective whose trust in evidence becomes exploitable. Clara Cole — Ethan's sister, trapped between synchronized branches. Jonah Reed, Lena Voss and Silas Ward each represent competing interpretations of AHEAD.

## Non-negotiable continuity rules
A prediction may show a true image while hiding causal context. Character knowledge must never advance earlier than the EN-v5 authority. AHEAD and Return remain proper system/protocol names. Every episode must work as a cold entry while rewarding serial viewing. Every episode must end on an unresolved forward-driving beat.

## Localization rule
Preserve intent, tension and knowledge state; do not translate syntax literally. Dialogue must sound native at the language-specific WPM target.
## V5 continuity authority

- Maya is **Version 17**, not Receiver 17; early AHEAD labels intentionally obscure the namespace.
- **Leila Hart** is the parking-garage woman from E002/E003 and Receiver 16. Silas briefly disconnected her relay, which explains her hospital disappearance before AHEAD recaptured her.
- **Adrian** survives after E007 because Silas disconnects his relay; he returns in E070 as living evidence that disconnection works.
- Clara explicitly refuses to become the moral excuse for preserving AHEAD.
- E097–E099 resolve the season through rules and voluntary choice: Maya receives one final seven-minute prediction, deletes it before seeing the ending, and chooses the anchor without predictive authority.
