# Editorial QA — Deutsch

**Review persona:** Native German vertical-microdrama showrunner + retention editor + cross-language continuity supervisor.

## Gates

- First-second hook / cold-entry clarity
- Curiosity pressure and emotional conflict
- Native dialogue naturalness
- Escalation and reversal quality
- Hard cliffhanger
- Canonical continuity preservation
- Runtime: 140–155 spoken words at 150 WPM (~56–62s)
- Editorial weighted score >= 9.50/10

Scores are editorial quality judgments, not empirical guarantees of views or virality.
