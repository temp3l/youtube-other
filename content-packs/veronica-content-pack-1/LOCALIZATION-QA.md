# Localization QA

All five locales were reviewed for spoken naturalness, semantic fidelity, title strength, and shared-visual compatibility.

## Policy

- English is the canonical semantic master.
- German, Italian, French, and Portuguese are naturalized rather than translated word-for-word.
- No locale may introduce a new factual claim that would require a different image.
- The semantic beat order is preserved.
- Generated images must not contain readable language-specific text.
- Localized text is added as subtitles or render-time overlays.

## Locale notes

### German
Improved several literal or noun-heavy constructions and normalized YouTube-facing titles. The conversational `du` register remains consistent.

### Italian
Preserves the source corpus’s predominantly female-oriented address where natural, while avoiding unnecessary gender marking in some hooks. Phrasing is optimized for spoken delivery rather than transcript fidelity.

### French
Several constructions were rewritten away from literal English syntax. `vous` remains consistent and titles are now more idiomatic for YouTube.

### Portuguese
Kept Brazilian-leaning neutral Portuguese, shortened literal constructions, and strengthened hook cadence.

### English
Optimized as the canonical retention master. The other locales follow its semantic beats, not its exact sentence lengths.
