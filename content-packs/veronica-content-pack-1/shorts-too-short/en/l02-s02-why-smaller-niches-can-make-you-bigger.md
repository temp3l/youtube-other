Choosing a smaller niche can feel like throwing customers away. But early positioning often needs the opposite of breadth. A specific niche gives people a simple reason to remember you. It makes your examples sharper, your content easier to plan, and your offer easier to understand. Once the market knows what you are the person for, you can expand from a position of strength. Starting broad is harder because nobody knows which part of your expertise to remember first. A niche gives you a foothold. It does not have to define your entire future.
