You can change your profile description in thirty seconds. Repositioning takes longer because you are not editing text — you are updating memory. Your offer, website, content, examples, conversations, and professional story all need to reinforce the new direction. Some people will understand immediately. Others will keep seeing the old version of you for a while. That overlap is normal. The mistake is changing direction again because the market did not update fast enough. A new bio announces the position. Repeated evidence makes the position believable and memorable.
