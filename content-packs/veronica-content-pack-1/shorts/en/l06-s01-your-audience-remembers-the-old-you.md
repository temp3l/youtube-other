Your business can change faster than your audience’s memory. You may have moved into a new field, built new skills, and created a new offer, while people still associate you with what you did two years ago. That does not mean the new direction is wrong. It means the market’s mental model has not caught up yet. Repositioning starts by identifying the association that already exists, then deliberately building a new one. You are not just changing a service. You are changing what problem should make people think of your name.
