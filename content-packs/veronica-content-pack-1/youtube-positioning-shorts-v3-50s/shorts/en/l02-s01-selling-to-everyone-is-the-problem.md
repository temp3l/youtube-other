If your offer is for everyone, your message usually becomes relevant to no one. Different customers have different needs, fears, priorities, and reasons for buying. The more generic your message becomes, the more work the customer has to do to decide whether it applies to them. A niche solves that problem. It does not mean nobody outside the niche can buy from you. It means one group can immediately recognize, “This is for me.” Specificity is not a restriction. Compare ‘I help businesses grow’ with ‘I help independent service professionals turn expertise into an offer clients understand quickly.’ The second statement excludes some people, but it also removes work from the right buyer. They can recognize themselves, the problem, and the promised direction immediately. That is why a narrower message can create more qualified attention than a broader one. It is a communication advantage. Before making your offer broader, ask whether making it clearer would work better.
