Want people to know you for something new? Do not start by posting more. Start by defining the old association. What do people think of when they hear your name today? Then define the new expertise, explain the bridge from old to new, and align your offer, site, content, and collaborations around it. Finally, listen to the language people use. When inquiries and introductions begin using the new category, repositioning is working. Make the bridge explicit. Tell people what the old work taught you, which skills transferred, and what problem pulled you into the new field. Then make the new association visible everywhere for long enough to register. A useful measurement is the language of incoming inquiries. When people stop asking mainly about the old topic and begin introducing you with the new category, the market’s memory is actually changing. The goal is not more content. It is a different association.
