You do not need a major publisher to use a book as an authority asset. A focused book can be self-published, released as an ebook, or even given away. The value is not the logo on the cover. The value is that you have organized useful knowledge around the expertise you want people to associate with your name. A seventy-page guide that solves one important problem can position you more clearly than a two-hundred-page book that tries to prove how much you know. Imagine a prospect hears your name, receives the book from a colleague, and finds a useful framework inside. Now your expertise has something tangible attached to it. The book works because it makes the knowledge easier to consume, share, and connect back to you. Publishing does not create expertise. But a strong book gives existing expertise a durable object people can read, recommend, reference, and remember.
