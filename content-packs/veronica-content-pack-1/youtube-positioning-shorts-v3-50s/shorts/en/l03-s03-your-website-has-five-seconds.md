Open your website and pretend you have never seen your business before. Can you understand, within five seconds, who it is for and what problem it solves? If not, adding more sections may not help. Positioning needs a clear first impression. Your website does not have to explain your entire career on the first screen. It needs to give the visitor the right category. Who do you help? With what? Try a brutal test: show only the first screen to someone who does not know you, then hide it and ask three questions. Who is this for? What do they help with? Why should I continue? If the answers are vague, the page has made the visitor do interpretation work. Design cannot rescue a missing category. Clarity first gives the rest of the site something useful to amplify. Why should they keep reading? A simple one-page site with a coherent message can position you better than a beautiful site that leaves people guessing.
