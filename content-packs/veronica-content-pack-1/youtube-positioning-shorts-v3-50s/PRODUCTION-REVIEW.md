## Editorial verdict
The strongest production candidates are **L03-S03, L04-S02, L06-S01, and L06-S02** because they combine an immediate tension point with a concrete diagnostic or behavior change and a clean final takeaway.

No Short is rejected editorially. The remaining risk is **duration calibration**, especially German, because the supplied renders show materially faster realized speech than the old 150-WPM estimator assumed. Do not solve duration misses by slowing the voice unnaturally. If a measured render falls below 40 seconds, expand the affected localization with one additional concrete example or consequence and regenerate TTS only.

## Visual production recommendation
For the longer scripts, target **7–9 semantic visual beats** rather than stretching the existing five images. Reuse existing visual assets only where the semantic beat still matches. Do not regenerate images merely because a localization changes duration.
