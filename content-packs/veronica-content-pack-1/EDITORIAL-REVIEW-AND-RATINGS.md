## Long-form findings

### L01
Strong foundational concept, but the original opening took too long to reach the commercial consequence. The optimized version opens with losing the client despite superior expertise and reaches the expertise/perception gap immediately.

### L02
The original was structurally sound. The optimized version makes the counter-intuitive cost of broad positioning explicit in the first seconds while preserving the market → problem → solution framework.

### L03
The original had good advice but an essay-like middle. The optimized version sharpens the recognition gap and adds a concrete UX-designer-to-conversion-consultant example as a mid-video pattern interrupt.

### L04
Already one of the strongest topics. The optimized version makes the memory problem explicit immediately and adds a concrete nutrition-consultant example to reduce abstraction.

### L05
The original title slightly overclaimed what a book can do. The optimized version explicitly says a book does not create expertise; it makes real expertise more visible, and adds a practical content-reuse chain.

### L06
The strongest narrative conflict in the series. The optimized opening now starts directly with the mismatch between a changed business and slower audience memory.
