A positioning book is not only a finished product. It can become months of coherent content. A chapter can become several Shorts. A framework can become a diagram. A client question can become an article. An example can become an interview topic. The important part is not the number of pieces. Take one strong chapter. Its central mistake can become a Short. Its framework can become a diagram. A client example can become a newsletter. A common objection can become an interview question. A checklist can become a carousel. You are not chopping a book into fragments just to fill a calendar. You are distributing the same intellectual territory repeatedly, which makes the content easier to produce and the positioning easier to remember. It is that every piece reinforces the same expertise. Instead of inventing random content every week, you distribute one coherent body of knowledge across different formats.
