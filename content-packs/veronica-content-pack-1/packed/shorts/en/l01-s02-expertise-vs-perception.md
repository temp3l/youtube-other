Getting better at your craft will not fix this problem: the market may still have no idea how good you are. Expertise lives in the work. Positioning lives in the customer’s mind. Those are two different systems. Improve your skills, yes — but also make the evidence easy to see. Your offer, examples, content, reputation, and results should all point toward the same expertise. Think of your public presence as an evidence system. If your bio says one thing, your case studies show another, and your content jumps between unrelated topics, the buyer has to assemble the story for you. Most people will not. But when the same expertise appears in your offer, examples, explanations, and results, the conclusion becomes almost automatic: this person understands my problem. Never fake authority. But do not hide real authority behind a message nobody can understand.
