If you constantly ask, “What should I post today?”, you probably have a content problem caused by a positioning problem. Start somewhere else: what do you want people to remember you for? Choose one core expertise. List the recurring problems inside it. Then turn each problem into explanations, mistakes, examples, questions, stories, and conversations. Now your content can vary without your professional identity changing every day. Suppose your core expertise is pricing for independent consultants. One week you can explain a common pricing mistake. Next week, show a client scenario. Then answer an objection, break down a decision, react to a bad example, or tell a story about scope creep. Those posts are different enough to stay interesting, but similar enough to strengthen the same memory. Variety should happen inside the position, not instead of it. Good positioning gives content boundaries. Those boundaries are useful because every new post can strengthen an association that already exists instead of starting from zero.
