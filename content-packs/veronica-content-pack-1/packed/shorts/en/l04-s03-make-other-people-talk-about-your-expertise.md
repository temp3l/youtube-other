One of the strongest positioning signals happens when you are not in the room. Someone asks, “Do you know anybody who can help with this?” and another person says your name. Content can help create that moment. Explain your field clearly, interview people from adjacent areas, participate in relevant conversations, and make your knowledge easy to share. The goal is not only to tell people what you do. You can design for referrals by giving people language they can repeat. ‘She does marketing’ is weak. ‘She helps boutique hotels turn direct-booking data into better offers’ is much easier to pass on. Your content should repeatedly supply that kind of sentence through examples and explanations. When your audience can describe your relevance accurately without checking your profile, you have created a portable position. It is to build such a clear association between you and a problem that other people can repeat the association for you. That is when positioning starts becoming reputation.
