Starting from zero? Build these three signals before you chase followers. First: a clear offer. People should understand the problem you solve. Second: useful content. Let them see how you think before you ask them to buy. Third: relevant context. Show up where the people and conversations in your field already exist. You do not need a huge audience or a complex website. A simple first month could look like this: one sharply defined offer, three recurring content themes around that problem, and regular participation in two places where the right people already talk about it. That is enough to start creating a pattern. The objective is not to appear everywhere. It is to make every early signal teach the market the same association, so recognition compounds instead of resetting with every post. At the beginning, alignment beats volume. Ten coherent signals around one expertise can build more recognition than fifty unrelated posts.
