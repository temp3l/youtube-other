Changing your bio to “expert” does not make the market see you as one. Recognition comes from evidence. Does your offer match the expertise you claim? Does your content show that you understand the problem? Does your website make your role clear? Are you present in conversations where that expertise matters? Positioning is the accumulation of these signals. You do not need to look huge or famous. For example, if you want to be known for conversion strategy, do not rely on the word ‘expert.’ Publish a teardown that shows what you notice, explain a decision you made, show an outcome, and let clients describe the result in their own words. None of those signals is dramatic alone. Together they reduce uncertainty. Authority becomes believable when the evidence lets the audience reach the conclusion without being instructed to. You need enough coherent evidence for people to reach the conclusion themselves. The strongest form of expert positioning is when other people start using the label before you do.
