You can change your profile description in thirty seconds. Repositioning takes longer because you are not editing text — you are updating memory. Your offer, website, content, examples, conversations, and professional story all need to reinforce the new direction. Some people will understand immediately. Others will keep seeing the old version of you for a while. That overlap is normal. The mistake is changing direction again because the market did not update fast enough. Think of every touchpoint as a vote for either the old position or the new one. If the bio changes but the homepage, examples, conversations, and next ten posts still reinforce the old category, the old category wins. Repositioning starts to move when the evidence becomes consistent enough that people repeatedly encounter the new story. That is why patience matters, but consistency matters more. Time alone does not update the market; repeated evidence does. A new bio announces the position. Repeated evidence makes the position believable and memorable.
