# Veronica Shorts — 90-Second Timing Policy

## Target

All Veronica Shorts should **target 01:30**, not merely remain below 01:30.

## Pre-TTS planning

| Locale | WPM | Exact 01:30 word target | Preferred planning band |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| DE | 150 | 225 | 218–233 |
| EN | 155 | ~233 | 225–240 |
| FR | 155 | ~233 | 225–240 |
| IT | 155 | ~233 | 225–240 |
| PT | 155 | ~233 | 225–240 |

The preferred band corresponds to approximately 01:27–01:33.

Word count is not a quality goal. Do not add redundant restatements to hit the target. Extra duration should carry useful examples, mechanisms, consequences, diagnostics, contrast, or payoff.

## Post-TTS authority

Once selected narration audio exists:

1. selected-audio duration is canonical;
2. WPM estimates become advisory only;
3. evaluate actual pauses, emphasis, sentence cadence, and intelligibility;
4. retime or edit narration if the selected audio materially misses the ~01:30 target;
5. downstream visual timing must derive from the selected audio, not the planning word count.
