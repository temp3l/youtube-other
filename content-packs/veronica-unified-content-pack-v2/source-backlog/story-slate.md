# 100-Story YouTube Slate

**Editorial scoring** is a prioritization heuristic based on hook strength, evergreen demand, source depth, clarity, and fit with an expert-business YouTube channel. It is not a forecast of views.

## Production targets

- Shorts: **90 seconds**, **155 WPM**, target **232 words**, tolerance ±12 words.
- Long-form: **10 minutes**, **155 WPM**, target **1,550 words**, tolerance ±75 words.
- Canonical concept language: English. Source material: Italian. Localization should happen only after the canonical script passes editorial QA.
- No transcript may be copied verbatim into narration. Reconstruct ideas, preserve provenance, and independently verify externally checkable claims/examples before publication.
