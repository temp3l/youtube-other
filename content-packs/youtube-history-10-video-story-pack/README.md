# YouTube History Channel — 10-Video Documentary Story Pack

## Writer persona

**Cinematic Public Historian — a documentary writer who combines narrative tension, historiographical caution, primary-source awareness, and accessible explanation.**

The scripts use a cinematic but non-fiction-first style:

- Open with a specific human-scale moment.
- Explain systems, incentives, and decisions rather than relying on “great man” mythology.
- Distinguish evidence, interpretation, and unresolved debate.
- Avoid invented dialogue and unsupported internal thoughts.
- Use restrained language for death, violence, and human suffering.
- End with a durable historical insight rather than a generic call to action.

## Runtime assumptions

Scripts target a measured history-documentary pace of roughly 104–112 spoken words per minute, including deliberate pauses and visual breathing room. Word counts and duration estimates are stored in each file’s YAML metadata.

## File structure

Each Markdown file contains:

1. YAML production metadata
2. Chapter timestamps
3. Voiceover-ready documentary story
4. Visual and thumbnail direction
5. Fact-check notes
6. Research sources

## Historical scope note

Several topics remain debated among historians. These scripts explicitly avoid presenting contested single-cause explanations as settled fact.
