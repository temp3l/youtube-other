# S004 — Stop Saying “I Help Everyone”

## Production spec

- Format: **Short**
- Target: **1:30** at **155 WPM**
- Narration target: **232 words** (±12)
- Pillar: **Positioning**
- Editorial priority score: **9.6/10**

## Hook

The sentence ‘I can help anyone’ sounds ambitious. To a buyer, it often sounds like ‘I am for no one in particular.’

## Core angle

Broad targeting weakens relevance and makes communication generic.
