## Hook

Most educational content loses viewers for the same reason bad classrooms lose students: the information arrives without tension.

## Core angle

Fuse instruction with narrative beats, emotion, examples and pacing.

## Script construction requirements

- Open on the conflict or counterintuitive claim; no greeting or channel intro.
- Build a new narrative from the source ideas; do not paraphrase one transcript sequentially.
- Translate source concepts into natural English rather than literal Italian structure.
- Use concrete examples and a visible cause → consequence chain.
- Preserve one dominant thesis. Remove course-specific promotion, live-session chatter, and obsolete context.
- Do not invent quotations, statistics, case-study outcomes, or named-person facts.
- End on the payoff/implication, not a generic summary.
