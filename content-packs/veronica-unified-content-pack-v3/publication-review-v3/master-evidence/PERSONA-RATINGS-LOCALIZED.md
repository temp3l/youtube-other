# Localized Persona Ratings

All 270 non-English narrations were re-rated using five AI editorial lenses:

1. Native-language / spoken naturalness — 25%
2. Semantic fidelity to canonical English — 25%
3. Retention / pacing — 20%
4. Business clarity / usefulness — 15%
5. Series coherence — 15%

Passing requires an overall score **strictly greater than 9.5**, timing PASS, and no critical hygiene defect.

> These are AI editorial ratings, not human native-speaker certification.
