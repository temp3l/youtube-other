# Locale Conventions

## Portuguese (`pt`)

The corpus is predominantly Brazilian Portuguese in direct-address usage. Evidence across the complete PT narration corpus:

- `você`: 658
- `seu`: 148
- `sua`: 103
- `tu`: 0
- `teu`: 0
- `tua`: 0

New and remediated PT narration follows the dominant PT-BR convention. Existing legitimate quoted or legacy variants were not globally rewritten solely to force pronoun uniformity.

## French (`fr`)

The established narrator register is predominantly formal/plural direct address (`vous`). Evidence across the complete FR narration corpus:

- `vous`: 869
- `votre`: 377
- `vos`: 62
- `tu`: 6
- `ton`: 4

All newly authored or v4-resynchronized French narration introduced in this remediation uses the established `vous` narrator register. Remaining isolated `tu`/`ton` tokens are from pre-existing corpus text and were not mechanically rewritten without contextual evidence.
