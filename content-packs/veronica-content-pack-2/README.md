# Veronica Content Pack 2 — Offer, Sales & Conversion

YouTube-optimized multilingual content pack.

## Primary content layout

- `long/{it,en,de,es,pt}/*.md` — 8 long-form narrations per language
- `shorts/{it,en,de,es,pt}/*.md` — 16 Shorts per language
- `metadata/{it,en,de,es,pt}/*.json` — localized YouTube packaging for long-form episodes and linked Shorts
- `mock-audio/long/{lang}/*.m4a` — duration fixtures only
- `mock-audio/shorts/{lang}/*.m4a` — duration fixtures only
- `support/` — QA, ratings, timing reports, semantic masters and source material

## Timing targets

- Long-form: 9:30–10:30
- Shorts: 1:00–1:30
- English Shorts: target 155 WPM; preferred 145–165; soft 135–175

Mock audio is explicitly non-publishable. Real selected TTS audio becomes the canonical timing authority before production.
