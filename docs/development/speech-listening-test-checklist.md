# Multilingual speech listening test

Use a fixed, version-controlled script per language containing ordinary narration, names,
numbers, dates, abbreviations, emotional emphasis, questions, long and short sentences,
foreign terms, and known pronunciation edges. Do not use private source material.

Record: `profileVersionId`, language, reviewer, date, intelligibility, voice similarity,
pronunciation, pacing, stability, audio artifacts, decision, and notes. Compare the raw
artifact and canonical master on headphones and representative phones/speakers. Require
an explicit approval record before the version can become a genre default.
