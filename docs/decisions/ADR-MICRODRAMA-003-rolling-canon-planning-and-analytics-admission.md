# ADR-MICRODRAMA-003: Rolling canon planning and analytics admission

Date: 2026-08-12
Status: accepted

## Context

A 100-episode season needs long-range coherence without pre-producing every
episode. Performance evidence can improve future presentation, but direct
analytics-to-canon mutation would corrupt established truth and chronology.

## Decision

Plan at four horizons: season macro, bounded arc, near horizon, and current
production episode. Accepted snapshots, open promises, reveal permissions, and
episode boundaries constrain every planning revision. Only the current bounded
production scope may trigger expensive downstream work.

Analytics observations and experiment results are immutable, revision-linked
evidence. They may create CreativeRecommendation revisions. A recommendation
becomes planning input only after canon-aware validation and explicit acceptance;
it never writes accepted canon directly.

## Consequences

- Future pacing, hook presentation, visuals, metadata, and canon-safe revisions
  can learn from evidence.
- Prior events, truth, knowledge history, reveal order, and promise resolution
  remain protected.
- Cross-locale/platform comparisons remain observational unless controlled
  assignment supports causal inference.
- Season boundaries prevent the E100 Season 2 hook from rewriting Season 1.

## Alternatives rejected

- Writing/rendering the whole season before observing production evidence.
- Analytics or an LLM mutating canon automatically.
- Treating raw views or editorial craft scores as causal performance truth.
- Unbounded rolling replanning without accepted snapshot constraints.
