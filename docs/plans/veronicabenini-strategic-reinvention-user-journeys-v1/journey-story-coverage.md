## Coverage expectation

A journey is considered implementation-ready only when its P0 stories are planned and every cross-cutting requirement relevant to the involved stages has an explicit owner. P1/P2 stories may be deferred, but deferral must not weaken invariants around revision immutability, tenant isolation, idempotency, auditability, source lineage, or safe regeneration.
