# VJ-13 — Analyze Performance and Iterate

## Primary actor
Creator / Channel Owner

## Main flow
1. Published platform metrics are imported or entered.
2. Metrics are linked to the exact production/publishing revision.
3. Creator compares episodes, formats, languages, and editorial strategies.
4. System may suggest future changes to pacing, visual density, hooks, length, or localization priorities.
5. Suggestions never mutate approved/published episodes automatically.
6. Creator can fork a new revision or new episode from a successful pattern.
7. Historical metrics remain attached to the version that produced them.

## Success outcome
Performance feedback informs future production without corrupting historical lineage.
