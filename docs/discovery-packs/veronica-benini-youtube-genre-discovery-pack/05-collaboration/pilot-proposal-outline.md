# Pilot Proposal Outline

## Working title

**Veronica Benini — Human-Led Multilingual YouTube Pilot**

## Proposition

Transform approved, human-authored Veronica material into high-quality Italian
long-form videos and Shorts, then localize proven evergreen episodes for international
audiences—without replacing her authorship, exposing premium content or requiring
constant social presence.

## Pilot deliverables

- creator and rights onboarding;
- 12 Italian flagship videos;
- 36 Italian Shorts;
- localized versions of the four strongest evergreen episodes;
- English and Spanish first;
- reviewed subtitles, audio tracks and metadata;
- source provenance and approval audit trail;
- analytics and production-efficiency report.

## Veronica supplies

- approved source recordings or writing;
- voice recording where agreed;
- brand assets;
- topic and story permissions;
- script and render approvals;
- CTA and offer information.

## Production system supplies

- transcription and source structuring;
- episode blueprints;
- editing and Short extraction;
- visual planning;
- asset production;
- captions;
- rendering;
- localization;
- metadata;
- upload package;
- provenance and approvals;
- pilot analytics.

## Explicit exclusions

- no autonomous first-person ghostwriting;
- no unapproved book adaptation;
- no scraping of paid/private material;
- no voice clone or avatar without separate permission;
- no automatic publishing;
- no automatic use of current sensitive personal events.

## Commercial structure to negotiate

Recommended pilot structure:

- fixed setup fee;
- fixed per-approved-episode production fee;
- pass-through external model/render costs;
- optional performance bonus tied to agreed conversions;
- no perpetual claim over her name, voice, likeness or creator profile;
- reusable genre remains part of Mediaforge;
- creator-specific data is deleted or archived only as contractually required after
  termination.

## Decision after 90 days

Scale only when the pilot demonstrates:

- authentic voice;
- measurable time savings;
- qualified audience growth;
- functioning public-to-owned funnel;
- reliable localization;
- low editorial and rights risk.
