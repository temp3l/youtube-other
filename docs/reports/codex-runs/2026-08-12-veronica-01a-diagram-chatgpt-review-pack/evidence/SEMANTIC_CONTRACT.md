# S02-B01 semantic and visual contract

## Narration

“Start with what the customer pays. Then subtract production, payment fees, commissions, shipping, support, refunds, and every cost that increases when you sell one more unit. What remains tells you much more than the order count.”

The candidate specifically covers: “Then subtract production, payment fees, commissions, shipping, support, refunds, and every cost that increases when you sell one more unit.”

## Required meaning

One sale enters at full value. Successive variable costs visibly remove value. One strictly smaller retained remainder exits. The causal direction must never imply that costs create value or that the output exceeds the input.

## Must show

- exactly one input sale/value block;
- one continuous shrinking value path;
- separated cost-removal stations;
- one removed fragment per station;
- exactly one final remainder, visibly smaller than input;
- generic human action without identifiable likeness.

## Must not show

- multiple independent sale blocks or duplicated input value;
- output material exceeding input;
- unaccounted decorative value blocks;
- readable text, labels, arrows, numbers, currency symbols, logos, or UI;
- synthetic Veronica likeness or portrait-led composition;
- occupation-specific or generic business-stock imagery.

## Format and continuity

Native 9:16, 864×1536. Subtitle-safe region: x=0.12, y=0.72, width=0.76, height=0.16. Palette: warm paper, coral accent, ink, glass, unfinished wood. The preceding S01-B02 beat shows the customer/payment handoff; S02-B01 must be materially distinct and advance to variable-cost subtraction.

## Failure history

The latest photorealistic candidate cleared numeric thresholds (semantic 0.84, instant-read 0.77, narration support 0.86) but strict must-show coverage remained partial: several upright coral blocks made the intended single continuously shrinking path ambiguous. The deterministic candidate exists to make that relationship geometrically explicit.
