# Episode 025 — The Endless Backrooms

## Narration Script
Arin Caldor reached the service entrance after midnight and heard the fluorescent hum before he saw the red door.
Wet carpet soaked through his shoes while an internal phone rang behind the service corridor wall.
A note beside the red door said never answer the phone unless the extension repeats your own number.
He ignored the first call, opened the red door, and found another service hall folded into the backrooms below the building.
The final underground level was not a basement at all but a buried city of rooms built around the same ringing extension.
When the phone rang one last time, the display showed Arin's extension calling from underneath his feet.
